Horror Pack: 3-8 friends on a paid server (ziggy.dathost.net:17772). Story: years ago "the Fall" emptied the great
cities; their trains and machines still run. Players explore ruined Lost Cities, rebuild with Create, rescue survivors,
and survive horror mobs at night. Quest book (FTB Quests, in inventory) holds the story: Arrival, The Platform,
Below the Line, Lights Out, Other Doors, Above.

Rules and settings: keepInventory is ON. Difficulty easy. Flying allowed (airships, jetpacks). Spawn is shared
(spawnRadius 6). Weather cycle is off (no thunderstorms). First 3 in-game days after a new world: horror mobs don't spawn (grace period). Kenny (a stalker
boss) starts hunting on day 6. Weeping Angels: don't look away;
any weapon hurts them; their touch can teleport you up to 64 blocks.

Controls: B backpack, M world map, U waypoints, Y minimap settings, ` (backtick) hold to vein-mine any block,
\ toggle vein-mining, O vein-mine pattern, K toggle shaders (turn off if Ponder goes black), J Ad Astra suit flight,
V voice chat menu. Trash slot in the inventory. Inventory sort button (Inventory Profiles Next).
Trees: break one log of a natural tree and it all falls (sneak to break one log).

COLONY (MineColonies): the players' town is "Bonertopolis" (colony 1, mayor New_Indixico, officer ItsWeabooJones), next to spawn
(-2144, 96, -606, world "world_frost"). Huts are blocks; a Builder builds/upgrades them from work orders. Workers post
requests for items they need (speech bubble; right-click the hut to see them).
THE FOREMAN (/foreman <anything>, or say "foreman, ..." out loud on voice chat): a smarter AI (Opus) that runs the colony on
request. It explains what's going on, unsticks builders, queues builds/upgrades/repairs, places huts, runs commands, and can
take on a longer job ("get a second residence built") and check back every few minutes. It only acts when asked.
/foreman chest (while looking at a chest) = make it a SUPPLY CHEST: every 30 s, items workers are waiting for are taken from
supply chests and handed to them. Only marked chests are used. /foreman jobs, /foreman stop.
THE CREW: up to 3 survivors the foreman can hire ("foreman, hire two crew") who run errands for the colony: gather wood/stone/
sand/crops, dig, fetch, craft, small builds. What they bring goes into the supply chest. Talk to a crew member by saying
their name (or looking at them while talking); the foreman answers and gives the orders. They don't wander or act alone.
Whole crew at once: start with "everyone," or "crew," (voice or chat), e.g. "everyone, go chop wood": they all answer
together ("Roger!") and scramble. "crew, stop" halts them instantly. More crew join as the colony grows, and sometimes by
chance (someone at the gate, or met on the road). /foreman subtitles off hides their chat lines (you still hear them).
/foreman protect [radius] keeps an area safe from the crew and the foreman; /foreman skills lists saved routines.
If a player asks how to get something done in the colony, the short answer is usually: ask the foreman.
Old survivors/Settlement Beacons are retired (random survivors no longer spawn).

Big mods: Create (+ Aeronautics airships, Steam 'n' Rails trains, Big Cannons, Crafts & Additions, Power Loader),
Lost Cities (+ Create subways), Ad Astra (space: Moon, Mars...), Twilight Forest, the Aether, the Undergarden,
Mowzie's Mobs, YUNG's dungeons, When Dungeons Arise, Towns & Towers, Dungeons & Taverns, Lootr, Sophisticated
Backpacks, Xaero's minimap + world map (shared between players), Distant Horizons, Simple Voice Chat, JEI, Jade,
FallingTree, VeinMiner, TrashSlot, Guard Villagers, Easy NPC, KubeJS. Horror: Weeping Angels, Cave Dweller,
The Knocker, Kenny (toned down), TheWatcher (its fake "Reality desynced" crash screen is a scare, not a real crash),
ArPhEx, Nyf's Spiders, Server-Side Horror, The Boy and the Bath, The Backwoods, SCP: Ultimate (SCPs; some explode),
Spore + Spore Inquisition (infection, slowed), Infection Nexus.
Shaders: Complementary Reimagined (on by default).
Useful commands for ops: /time set day, /weather clear, /tp, /gamerule, /spark, /foreman, /report <problem>.

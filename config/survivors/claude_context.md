Horror Pack: 3-8 friends on a paid server (ziggy.dathost.net:17772). Story: years ago "the Fall" emptied the great
cities; their trains and machines still run. Players explore ruined Lost Cities, rebuild with Create, rescue survivors,
and survive horror mobs at night. Quest book (FTB Quests, in inventory) holds the story: Arrival, The Platform,
Below the Line, Lights Out, Other Doors, Above.

Rules and settings: keepInventory is ON. Difficulty easy. Flying allowed (airships, jetpacks). Spawn is shared
(spawnRadius 0). First 3 in-game days after a new world: horror mobs don't spawn (grace period). Kenny (a stalker
boss) starts hunting on day 6. Horror Faces vanish in daylight under open sky. Weeping Angels: don't look away;
any weapon hurts them; their touch can teleport you up to 64 blocks.

Controls: B backpack, M world map, U waypoints, Y minimap settings, ` (backtick) hold to vein-mine any block,
\ toggle vein-mining, O vein-mine pattern, K toggle shaders (turn off if Ponder goes black), J Ad Astra suit flight,
V voice chat menu. Trash slot in the inventory. Inventory sort button (Inventory Profiles Next).
Trees: break one log of a natural tree and it all falls (sneak to break one log).

Survivors (our own mod): people found wandering the ruins. Right-click: follow / wait. Give food to heal.
Craft a Settlement Beacon (lantern top; planks, campfire, planks; 3 cobblestone) and place it; survivors who follow
you within 12 blocks move in and stay nearby. Talk to a survivor by typing in chat near them (or say their name).

Big mods: Create (+ Aeronautics airships, Steam 'n' Rails trains, Big Cannons, Crafts & Additions, Power Loader),
Lost Cities (+ Create subways), Ad Astra (space: Moon, Mars...), Twilight Forest, the Aether, the Undergarden,
Mowzie's Mobs, YUNG's dungeons, When Dungeons Arise, Towns & Towers, Dungeons & Taverns, Lootr, Sophisticated
Backpacks, Xaero's minimap + world map (shared between players), Distant Horizons, Simple Voice Chat, JEI, Jade,
FallingTree, VeinMiner, TrashSlot, Guard Villagers, Easy NPC, KubeJS. Horror: Weeping Angels, Cave Dweller,
The Knocker, Kenny, Horror Faces, Spore + Spore Inquisition (infection), Infection Nexus.
Shaders: Complementary Reimagined (on by default).
Useful commands for ops: /time set day, /weather clear, /tp, /gamerule, /survivors spawn, /survivors list, /spark.
